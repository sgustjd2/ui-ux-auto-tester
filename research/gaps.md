# Gap Tracker

Every unresolved question, contradiction, incomplete area, licensing limit, access problem, or recorded assumption lives here until resolved. Never guess silently; write the gap. Resolution goes in the `resolution` column with the date, and material outcomes become ADRs.

Vocabulary (validated by `scripts/check-harness.mjs`):

- `type`: OPEN_QUESTION, CONTRADICTION, INCOMPLETE, LICENSE, ACCESS, ASSUMPTION
- `status`: OPEN, RESOLVED, DEFERRED (DEFERRED means a later phase decides; the `context` names the phase)
- `gap_id`: `GAP-NNN`, sequential, never reused. Cells must not contain the `|` character.
