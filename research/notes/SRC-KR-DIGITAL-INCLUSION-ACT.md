# SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT — Digital Inclusion Act (디지털포용법)

- Authority: Republic of Korea (National Assembly); Ministry of Science and ICT (과학기술정보통신부)
- Canonical URL: binding Korean text at https://www.law.go.kr/ (디지털포용법). Enforcement decree in progress at the government legislation portal (opinion.lawmaking.go.kr)
- Version / date: promulgated 21 January 2025; enforced 22 January 2026
- Source status: CURRENT
- Superseded by / supersedes: takes over the accessibility provisions removed from the Framework Act on Intelligent Informatization (SRC-KR-INTELLIGENT-INFO-ACT, Articles 46, 46-2, 47 deleted effective 22 January 2026)
- License / access: Korean statute (public)
- Verified on: 2026-09-02 (existence, promulgation and enforcement dates, competent ministry, and accessibility scope confirmed from the Korean government policy briefing at korea.kr; the binding legal text and exact accessibility article numbers were not read at law.go.kr this session)
- Tier: T1
- Domains served: LEGAL-KR

## Scope and applicability

- The new Korean framework law for digital inclusion, effective 22 January 2026, intended to ensure all citizens can access and use AI and digital technologies without discrimination. It is now the primary legal basis for the national information and communications accessibility (web, mobile, software) quality certification, administered with the Korea Digital Accessibility Promotion Agency (한국디지털접근성진흥원).
- Accessibility scope (confirmed at a high level): duties to guarantee accessibility for persons with disabilities and older people, including obligations on kiosk (무인정보단말기) operators and on manufacturers and lessors, and staged application to public and private providers. The web accessibility certification that recognizes KWCAG 2.2 conformance is administered under this Act.
- Jurisdiction: KR. Rule class LEGAL.

## Candidate rules

- One primary `KRLAW-*` (or a Digital-Inclusion-Act-specific prefix, to register in Phase 2) LEGAL rule: covered providers must ensure information and communications accessibility for persons with disabilities and older people, with KWCAG 2.2 and the mobile guideline as the technical means; jurisdiction KR. Crosswalks to SRC-KR-KWCAG22 and SRC-KR-MOBILE-A11Y.
- Secondary rules for kiosk accessibility and manufacturer/lessor duties, deferred to Phase 2 from the binding text.

## Cross-references

- KWCAG 2.2 (SRC-KR-KWCAG22) and the Korean mobile guideline (SRC-KR-MOBILE-A11Y): the technical standards recognized by the certification under this Act.
- Framework Act on Intelligent Informatization (SRC-KR-INTELLIGENT-INFO-ACT): the former home of the accessibility provisions this Act absorbed.
- Disability Discrimination Act (SRC-KR-DISABILITY-ACT): the parallel general anti-discrimination duty.
