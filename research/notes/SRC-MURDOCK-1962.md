## Scope and applicability

- The classic experimental source for the serial position effect. The abstract describes a recall curve with a steep primacy effect over the first few items of a list, an S-shaped recency effect over the last several items, and a flat middle, and suggests the shape may come from interference within the list.
- Interface relevance (this project's application): items placed first and last in a list or sequence are more likely to be remembered than items in the middle, which grounds heuristic checks on ordering navigation items, options, and key messages. The paper studied word-list recall, not interfaces; NN/g's primacy-effect video (SRC-NNG-ARTICLES) makes the interface application.
- Rule class HEURISTIC; never a compliance requirement.

## Candidate rules
