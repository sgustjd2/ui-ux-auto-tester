## Scope and applicability

- Accessibility requirements for a wide range of ICT: web pages, non-web and hybrid documents, software including mobile applications, hardware, communication products, kiosks and transaction machines, audiovisual technologies, and intelligent user agents (introduction, paraphrased). Fourteen clauses and eight annexes.
- Legal role, per the foreword: prepared to give one voluntary means of conforming to the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882), with Annex ZB and clause A.2 mapping it to that Directive; Annex ZA maps it to the Web Accessibility Directive (2016/2102), updating the former Annex A; Annex ZC covers other Directives referenced by the EAA. Presumption of conformity applies only once it is cited in the Official Journal under the relevant Directive.
- Jurisdiction: EU and EFTA. Platforms: web, pwa, ios, android, desktop, hardware.

## Significant changes since V3.2.1 (foreword, paraphrased)

- Clauses 9 (web), 10 (non-web documents), and 11 (software) updated to align with WCAG 2.2. The table of contents shows a WCAG 2.2 AAA informative clause (9.5) and WCAG 2.2 conformance requirements in Annex C.
- Real-time text requirements (clause 6.2) significantly revised and extended to total conversation.
- New Annex ZA (WAD), new Annex ZB (EAA), new clause A.2 (EAA conformance evaluation).
- Four national dates are printed (24 August 2026, 30 November 2026, 31 May 2027, 31 May 2028) against the labels date of adoption, doa, dop/e, and dow; the extracted layout misaligns labels and dates, so the exact pairing is to be confirmed before any rule cites a date.

## Candidate rules

- Same modelling as V3.2.1 (see SRC-ETSI-EN301549 note): LEGAL records per clause with `equivalent` links to the WCAG records they incorporate. For clauses 9 to 11 the incorporated version is now WCAG 2.2 (SRC-W3C-WCAG22), which removes the WCAG 2.1 dependency for EU audits once V4.1.1 is cited.
- Jurisdiction logic for Phase 2: until OJ citation, EU WAD audits cite V3.2.1 as the presumption-of-conformity standard and may report V4.1.1 as the state of the art; after citation, V4.1.1 becomes the default. The switch must be verified at the Official Journal, not assumed.
