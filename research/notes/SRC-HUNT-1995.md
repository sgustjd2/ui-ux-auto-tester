## Scope and applicability

- Anchors the Von Restorff (isolation) effect through an English-language analysis of the original, since the 1933 paper is in German and, as Hunt's abstract on the Springer page states, not available in English. The abstract states the effect (an item that differs from otherwise similar items is remembered better), notes that modern theory stresses perceptual salience and attention, and reports that von Restorff's own evidence showed salience is not necessary and that difference must be judged in the context of similarity.
- Interface relevance (this project's application): making the primary action or key message distinct from similar items helps it stand out and be remembered, but distinctiveness works relative to what surrounds it; if many items are made distinct, none is. This supports heuristic checks on single, clear emphasis (for example one primary button per view).
- Rule class HEURISTIC; never a compliance requirement.
