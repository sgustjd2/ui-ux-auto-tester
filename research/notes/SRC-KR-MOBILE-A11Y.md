## Scope and applicability

- The Korean national standard for the accessibility of mobile application content, so that persons with disabilities and older people can perceive, operate, and understand mobile app content. It is OS- and device-neutral. It underpins the national mobile application accessibility certification.
- Jurisdiction: KR. Platforms: ios, android (mobile apps). Rule class NORMATIVE when applied in Korea; legal force through the Korean accessibility laws.

## Structure

- Verified from the document: standard KS X 3253:2016, established 2016-10-20 by 방송통신표준심의회.
- Corroborated: 19 checkpoints (준수 항목) covering perception, operation, and understanding of mobile app content, structured analogously to the web guidelines but adapted to touch and mobile interaction.

## Candidate rules

- 19 candidate `KRMOBILE-*` rules (one per checkpoint), rule_class NORMATIVE, jurisdiction KR, category accessibility, platforms ios/android. They crosswalk to WCAG (via WCAG2ICT mobile application) and to the Korean web guideline where the concept is shared. Per-checkpoint extraction is deferred to Phase 2 from the standard PDF.

## Cross-references

- KWCAG 2.2 (SRC-KR-KWCAG22): the web counterpart in the same Korean standards family.
- WCAG2ICT (SRC-W3C-WCAG2ICT) and WCAG 2.2 (SRC-W3C-WCAG22): the international mapping for mobile application accessibility.
- Korean accessibility laws (SRC-KR-DISABILITY-ACT, SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT): give the mobile standard legal weight through certification.
