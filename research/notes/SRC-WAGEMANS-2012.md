## Scope and applicability

- A centennial review of Gestalt perceptual grouping and figure-ground organization. The abstract says it reviews the classical grouping principles (proximity, similarity, common fate, good continuation, closure, symmetry, parallelism) and newer ones (synchrony, common region, element and uniform connectedness), their role in contour integration, figure-ground principles, and the neural mechanisms, and evaluates what modern vision science adds.
- Interface relevance (this project's application): one peer-reviewed anchor for the grouping principles behind the Gestalt principles that `prd.md` §7.3 lists, including continuity and uniform connectedness, which NN/g covers only in short videos. Prägnanz is not in the abstract; the reviewer found it mentioned only in the manuscript's history section, so it is not anchored here. NN/g articles remain the practitioner anchors for how each principle applies to layout.
- Rule class HEURISTIC; never a compliance requirement.
