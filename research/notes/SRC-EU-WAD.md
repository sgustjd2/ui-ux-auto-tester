## Scope and applicability

- Binds public sector bodies (the State, regional and local authorities, bodies governed by public law, and their associations). Requires their websites and mobile applications to be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.
- Technical baseline: conformity with the harmonized standard EN 301 549 (SRC-ETSI-EN301549) gives a presumption of conformity. The current harmonized version is EN 301 549 V3.2.1, made the harmonized standard by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1339; its web chapter maps to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. (The directive's own text named the 2015 EN 301 549 version; the harmonized reference has since been updated.)
- Beyond the technical baseline, the directive requires each public sector body to publish an accessibility statement, provide a feedback mechanism, and be subject to member-state monitoring and reporting.
- Jurisdiction: EU (transposed by member states). Rule class LEGAL.

## Key dates (verified)

- Adopted 26 October 2016; transposition deadline 23 September 2018.
- Websites: new public sector websites from 23 September 2019; existing websites from 23 September 2020.
- Mobile applications: from 23 June 2021.

## Candidate rules

- One primary `WAD-*` LEGAL rule: public sector websites and mobile apps must conform to EN 301 549 (hence WCAG 2.1 Level AA) by the applicable date; it crosswalks (`equivalent`) to the EN 301 549 / WCAG rules, jurisdiction EU.
- Secondary `WAD-*` LEGAL rules for the procedural obligations the directive adds beyond WCAG: publish an accessibility statement, provide a feedback mechanism, and support monitoring. These have no WCAG equivalent and are the directive's distinct contribution.
- Extraction of the accessibility-statement model content (from the associated implementing decision) is deferred to Phase 2.

## Cross-references

- EN 301 549 (SRC-ETSI-EN301549): the harmonized standard giving presumption of conformity.
- European Accessibility Act (SRC-EU-EAA): the private-sector counterpart.
- WCAG 2.1 (to register per GAP-027): the web accessibility core reached through EN 301 549 V3.2.1.

## Uncertainties and gaps

- The harmonized EN 301 549 version can change by implementing decision; the WCAG version reached (2.1 now, 2.2 when V4.x is harmonized) must be re-checked before packaging.
- The accessibility-statement and monitoring specifics live in separate implementing decisions (for example (EU) 2018/1523 and (EU) 2018/1524); register them in Phase 2 if the procedural rules are normalized.
- The corpus records the directive, not each national transposition, unless specific jurisdictions are enabled (GAP-023).
