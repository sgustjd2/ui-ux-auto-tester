## Scope and applicability

- The Korean national web content accessibility standard, the KWCAG counterpart to WCAG. It is the standard used for the national web accessibility quality certification (정보통신접근성 품질인증), now administered under the Digital Inclusion Act (SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT).
- Jurisdiction: KR. Platforms: web, pwa. Rule class NORMATIVE (a national standard) when applied in Korea; LEGAL force comes through the Korean accessibility laws that reference it.

## Structure

- Verified from the document: four principles (인식의 용이성 Perceivable, 운용의 용이성 Operable, 이해의 용이성 Understandable, 견고성 Robust), matching the WCAG POUR model.
- Corroborated: 14 guidelines and 33 checkpoints (검사항목) distributed across the four principles. KWCAG 2.2 aligns closely with WCAG 2.x but is a distinct national standard with its own checkpoint set and numbering.
- Deliberation: 방송통신표준심의회 정보보호 기술심의회; original draft cooperation by TTA with participants from NIA (한국지능정보사회진흥원), Samsung, LG, and accessibility research bodies.

## Candidate rules

- 33 candidate `KWCAG-*` rules (one per checkpoint), rule_class NORMATIVE, jurisdiction KR, category accessibility, platforms web/pwa. Most crosswalk (`equivalent` or `overlaps`) to WCAG rules; KWCAG has its own checkpoint identifiers and a few Korea-specific emphases. Per-checkpoint extraction is deferred to Phase 2 from the standard PDF (paraphrase only).
- The checkpoint-to-WCAG crosswalk is a Phase 2 task; KWCAG 2.2's 33 checkpoints do not map one-to-one to WCAG's 86 criteria (KWCAG selects and adapts).

## Cross-references

- WCAG 2.2 (SRC-W3C-WCAG22): the international counterpart; KWCAG checkpoints crosswalk to WCAG criteria.
- Korean accessibility laws (SRC-KR-DISABILITY-ACT, SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT): give KWCAG conformance legal weight through the certification scheme.
- Korean mobile guideline (SRC-KR-MOBILE-A11Y): the mobile-app counterpart.
