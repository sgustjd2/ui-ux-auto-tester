# SRC-KR-INTELLIGENT-INFO-ACT — Framework Act on Intelligent Informatization (지능정보화 기본법)

- Authority: Republic of Korea (National Assembly); Ministry of Science and ICT
- Canonical URL: binding Korean text at https://www.law.go.kr/ (지능정보화 기본법). Official English reference translation at the Korea Legislation Research Institute, https://elaw.klri.re.kr/ (Framework Act on Intelligent Informatization)
- Version / date: wholly amended 9 June 2020 (renaming the former Framework Act on National Informatization); current version amended by Law No. 20410 of 26 March 2024, effective 27 March 2025. A consequential amendment accompanying the Digital Inclusion Act (Law No. 20672, promulgated 21 January 2025) deletes the accessibility articles effective 22 January 2026
- Source status: CURRENT (as a law), but its accessibility provisions were removed effective 22 January 2026 (see the key finding)
- Superseded by / supersedes: wholly amended from the Framework Act on National Informatization (SRC record not held). For accessibility, superseded by the Digital Inclusion Act (SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT)
- License / access: Korean statute (public); KLRI translation is a reference translation

- Tier: T1

## Key finding

The accessibility provisions this source was registered for have been deleted. The accessibility articles (46, 46-2, and 47, which guaranteed access to and use of intelligent information services by persons with disabilities and older people, and related certification) were deleted by the consequential amendment accompanying the Digital Inclusion Act (promulgated 21 January 2025), effective 22 January 2026. As of today (2026-09-02, past that date) they are removed. The Framework Act now carries only general digital-inclusion and digital-divide policy language, not the specific web/mobile accessibility duty. The accessibility mandate moved to the Digital Inclusion Act (SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT), effective 22 January 2026 (the same date the deletion took effect).

## Scope and applicability

- A framework law for national intelligent informatization policy. With the accessibility articles deleted effective 22 January 2026, it no longer holds the specific accessibility obligations; those live in the Digital Inclusion Act.
- Jurisdiction: KR. Rule class LEGAL, but it now yields no specific accessibility rule.

## Candidate rules

- None for accessibility, following the deletion of Articles 46, 46-2, and 47. The former Article 46 accessibility duty is now sourced from the Digital Inclusion Act.
- Retained here for historical continuity and because general digital-inclusion policy (Articles 44–45) provides context; no `KRLAW-*` accessibility rule is minted from this source.

## Cross-references

- Digital Inclusion Act (SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT): the current home of the accessibility provisions deleted here.
- Disability Discrimination Act (SRC-KR-DISABILITY-ACT): the parallel general anti-discrimination duty.

## Uncertainties and gaps

- The deletion of Articles 46, 46-2, and 47 is future-dated: promulgated 21 January 2025, effective 22 January 2026. A legal-database snapshot dated 31 January 2025 that still showed Article 46 is therefore consistent, not contradictory (the deletion had not yet taken effect). No contradiction remains; the only open item is reading the binding article text at law.go.kr (GAP-033).
- Because this source no longer carries accessibility rules, LEGAL-KR relies on the Disability Discrimination Act and the Digital Inclusion Act for accessibility.
