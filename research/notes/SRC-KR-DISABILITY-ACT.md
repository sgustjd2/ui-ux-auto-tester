## Scope and applicability

- The general Korean anti-discrimination law for persons with disabilities. It prohibits discrimination in access to and use of information, and requires provision of information and communication accessibility, including accessible websites and electronic information, by public and (in stages) private actors.
- Jurisdiction: KR. Rule class LEGAL.
- Accessibility provisions (corroborated): Article 20 (prohibition of discrimination in access to information) and Article 21 (duty to provide legitimate conveniences in information and communication, including accessible websites and electronic information). The article that mandates accessible websites is the legal basis for KWCAG conformance obligations.

## Candidate rules

- One primary `KRLAW-*` LEGAL rule: covered actors must not discriminate in access to information and must provide accessible websites and electronic information to persons with disabilities; it crosswalks (`equivalent`) to the KWCAG rules (SRC-KR-KWCAG22) as the technical means of compliance, jurisdiction KR.
- Secondary rules for the staged application to private actors and specific product/service duties (kiosks, mobile), deferred to Phase 2 from the binding text.

## Cross-references

- KWCAG 2.2 (SRC-KR-KWCAG22) and the Korean mobile guideline (SRC-KR-MOBILE-A11Y): the technical standards used to satisfy the accessibility duty.
- Digital Inclusion Act (SRC-KR-DIGITAL-INCLUSION-ACT): the newer framework administering accessibility certification.
- The international counterparts (WCAG, ADA, EN 301 549) for the US/EU crosswalk.
