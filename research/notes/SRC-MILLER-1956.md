## Scope and applicability

- The primary source behind "Miller's law". The abstract reviews research from an information-theory standpoint and concludes that an unaided observer is severely limited in how much information can be received, processed, and remembered, but that the bottleneck can be broken by using several stimulus dimensions, recoding, and mnemonic devices.
- Interface relevance (this project's application, used carefully as `prd.md` §7.3 requires): interfaces should not rely on users holding many items in memory, and chunking or recoding helps. The abstract does not support a fixed "at most seven items" rule for menus or navigation, a common misapplication; rules must not cite this paper for item-count limits.
- Rule class HEURISTIC; never a compliance requirement.
