## Scope and applicability

- The ten general usability heuristics for interface design, the anchor of the expert heuristic review layer (`prd.md` §7.3). They are broad rules of thumb, not compliance requirements.
- Jurisdiction: GLOBAL. Rule class HEURISTIC. Platforms all.

## Structure

The ten heuristics (names verbatim; descriptions paraphrased):

1. Visibility of system status — keep users informed with timely feedback.
2. Match between the system and the real world — use the users' language and familiar concepts.
3. User control and freedom — provide clearly marked exits, undo, and redo.
4. Consistency and standards — follow platform and industry conventions.
5. Error prevention — prevent problems before they occur.
6. Recognition rather than recall — make options visible to reduce memory load.
7. Flexibility and efficiency of use — offer accelerators for expert users.
8. Aesthetic and minimalist design — avoid irrelevant or rarely needed content.
9. Help users recognize, diagnose, and recover from errors — plain-language, specific error messages with a solution.
10. Help and documentation — provide help that is easy to search and task-focused.
