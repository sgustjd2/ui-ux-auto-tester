## Scope and applicability

- Companion to SRC-W3C-WCAG22. The page states that the Understanding documents are informative explanations, not required to meet WCAG, and not part of the normative standard.
- Use in this project: intent, benefits, examples, and testing guidance for each success criterion when normalizing rules and when writing `rationale`, `applicability`, and `expected_evidence`. Never a rule source; a rule may cite it only in `notes`.

## Structure

- Index organized by the four WCAG principles and thirteen guidelines, with one Understanding page per success criterion and one per guideline (each page follows the pattern of intent, benefits, examples, related resources, techniques, and failures).
- Additional entries: Introduction to Understanding WCAG 2.2, Understanding Techniques for WCAG 2.2 Success Criteria, Understanding Test Rules for WCAG 2.2 Success Criteria, Understanding Conformance, plus entries on referring to WCAG 2.2 from other documents, documenting accessibility support, and metadata.
- Identifier scheme: page slugs mirror the criterion fragment ids of the Recommendation (for example `target-size-minimum.html`).
- Normative vs informative: entirely informative.

## Candidate rules

None. This source produces no rule records. During Phase 2 each WCAG rule record should link the matching Understanding page in `notes` and draw `expected_evidence` from its testing guidance and listed failures.
