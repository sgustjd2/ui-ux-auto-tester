## Scope and applicability

- Sets common EU accessibility requirements for a defined list of products and services placed on the EU market. It applies from 28 June 2025 (products placed on the market and services provided after that date).
- Products: general-purpose consumer computer hardware and operating systems; self-service terminals (payment terminals, ATMs, ticketing and check-in machines, interactive information terminals); consumer terminal equipment for electronic communications and for audiovisual media services; e-readers.
- Services: electronic communications services; access to audiovisual media services; elements of air, bus, rail, and waterborne passenger transport; consumer banking services; e-books and dedicated software; e-commerce services; and answering emergency communications to 112.
- Jurisdiction: EU (transposed into national law by member states). Rule class LEGAL.

## Structure

- Functional accessibility requirements are set in Annex I (by product/service), with further annexes on examples, built environment (optional), conformity assessment, and criteria for disproportionate burden.
- Conformity: products and services in conformity with harmonized standards whose references are published in the Official Journal enjoy a presumption of conformity. The ICT harmonized standard for this purpose is the EN 301 549 family (SRC-ETSI-EN301549); a version aligned with the EAA (V4.x) is being developed under Commission standardization request M/587. Update 2026-09-23: EN 301 549 V4.1.1 (2026-09) is published by ETSI with Annex ZB and clause A.2 mapping it to this Directive, but it confers a presumption of conformity only once cited in the Official Journal, which had not happened at the check (SRC-ETSI-EN301549-V4).
- Normative vs informative: the articles and Annex I are binding; recitals and examples are explanatory.

## Candidate rules

- Candidate `EAA-*` LEGAL rules from the Annex I functional requirements (perceivability, operability, understandability, robustness of the covered products and services, plus information and support requirements), jurisdiction EU. Where a requirement is met through EN 301 549, the rule crosswalks (`equivalent`) to the EN 301 549 / WCAG rules.
- The digital-interface requirements (websites, mobile apps, e-commerce, e-books, self-service terminal UIs) crosswalk to the WCAG / EN 301 549 rules; the EAA adds the market-scope and the product categories (hardware, terminals) that WCAG does not cover.
- Extraction of Annex I item-by-item is deferred to Phase 2.

## Cross-references

- EN 301 549: V3.2.1 (SRC-ETSI-EN301549) and V4.1.1 (SRC-ETSI-EN301549-V4, published 2 September 2026 with Annex ZB for this Directive); presumption of conformity follows Official Journal citation (GAP-039).
- Web Accessibility Directive (SRC-EU-WAD): the public-sector counterpart; the EAA covers private products and services.
- WCAG (SRC-W3C-WCAG22): the web/app accessibility core reached through EN 301 549.
